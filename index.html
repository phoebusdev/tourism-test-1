Welcome to Voskopojë

Nestled high in the mountains of southeastern Albania at an elevation of 1,160 meters, Voskopojë (also known as Moscopole) is a village that seamlessly blends centuries of history with breathtaking natural beauty.

Located 21 kilometers from Korçë, Voskopojë stands as a testament to Albania's rich cultural heritage. Once known as the "Paris of the Balkans," this mountain village was one of the most important cultural and economic centers in the Ottoman Balkans during its golden age.

Today, Voskopojë offers visitors a unique opportunity to step back in time while enjoying the pristine natural environment of the Morava Mountains. The village is surrounded by lush forests, crystal-clear rivers, and abundant wildlife, making it an ideal destination for those seeking both cultural enrichment and outdoor adventure.

A Journey Through Time

The Rise and Transformation of the Paris of the Balkans

14th Century - Foundation

Voskopojë's history begins in the 14th century, establishing itself as a settlement in the mountainous region of southeastern Albania.

17th Century - Rise to Prominence

The village gained fame as a center of economic, cultural, and artistic development. During this period, Voskopojë became one of the biggest commerce and trade towns in the Balkans and beyond.

1600s-1800s - The Golden Age

At its height, the city had an estimated population between 30,000 and 60,000 residents. Voskopojë supported 26 Orthodox churches richly decorated with Byzantine frescoes, the only printing house in the Balkans outside Constantinople, and the renowned New Academy.

1760 - Paris of the Balkans

By 1760, Voskopojë had earned the prestigious title "Paris of the Balkans," reflecting its status as a major cultural, educational, and economic hub of the region.

Late 18th Century - Decline

Beginning in the late 18th century, Voskopojë was ransacked and razed three times in 20 years, marking the start of a tragic decline for this once-thriving city.

20th Century - Further Devastation

The village was burned during World War I and bombed in World War II, resulting in further loss of its architectural and cultural heritage.

21st Century - Preservation and Revival

Today, Voskopojë is experiencing a cultural renaissance. The Albanian Ministry of Culture has announced ambitious restoration plans, and tourism is helping to preserve and celebrate the village's unique heritage. Dedicated researchers and restorers are working to save the remaining churches and their magnificent frescoes.

Churches & Heritage

Voskopojë of the 24 Churches - A Testament to Byzantine Art

In history books, one might often come across the term "Voskopojë of the 24 churches." Of the more than 20 churches that once stood in the village, only six remain today, including five with magnificent frescoes.

Deep in southeastern Albania, this tiny hamlet holds five churches with one of the most magnificent concentrations of Orthodox Christian fresco art in the world. From the outside, the churches resemble stone barns, reflecting their 18th-century heritage as Christian gathering places in the Islamic Ottoman Empire. Inside, they reveal painted masterworks of brilliant blues, reds, and yellows.

⛪

St. Nicholas Church (St. Kolli)

Significance: Located in the center of Voskopojë, this is the most important church for its architecture, featuring all the elements of the post-Byzantine basilica type.

This church serves as a prime example of the architectural sophistication that characterized Voskopojë's golden age.

⛪

Virgin Mary Cathedral

Significance: One of the largest churches in Albania with a capacity of up to 1,000 worshippers.

This impressive cathedral demonstrates the scale and importance of Voskopojë as a religious and cultural center during its peak.

⛪

Church of the Archangels (St. Michael)

Built: Late 17th century

Frescoes: Painted circa 1722

A basilica type with curved cover and remarkable frescoes from the early 18th century, showcasing the artistic excellence of the period.

Preservation Efforts

As tourism in the area rises, dedicated researchers and restorers are racing to save the churches. The Albanian Ministry of Culture has announced an ambitious plan for restoration and development, including fortifying the masonry and sagging roofs of three churches.

Locals are shining a light on these structures, the village, and its history, ensuring that future generations can experience the magnificence of Voskopojë's cultural heritage.

Nature & Activities

A Paradise for Nature Lovers and Outdoor Enthusiasts

Nestled at an elevation of approximately 1,160 meters, Voskopojë offers stunning panoramic views of the surrounding Morava Mountains. The area is a paradise for nature lovers, featuring lush hiking trails, crystal-clear rivers, and abundant wildlife.

🥾

Hiking Trails

The area offers several hiking trails suitable for different levels of experience. For those who enjoy a leisurely stroll, there are short, scenic walks that allow you to admire the lush greenery and tranquil landscapes.

For more adventurous visitors, challenging routes lead to higher altitudes, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the village and the surrounding mountain ranges.

Featured Trail: The Voskopojë Pass leads visitors through pine forests to a spectacular viewpoint where the surrounding valley unfolds before you.
🌲

Village Walks

The easy-going hike to the neighboring village of Shipska is particularly interesting and provides a perfect escape into nature.

The landscape of Voskopojë and its surroundings is stunning, with every walk offering new perspectives on the natural beauty of the region.

🏞️

Natural Beauty

Surrounded by forests and the Morava Mountains, Voskopojë is a pleasant small mountain village offering visitors a rare blend of history, culture, and natural beauty.

The area features lush greenery, crystal-clear rivers, abundant wildlife, and a serene atmosphere perfect for relaxation and contemplation.

🌸

Seasonal Beauty

Spring: The hills come alive with vibrant blooms of wildflowers, creating a colorful carpet across the landscape.

Autumn: The hillsides are painted with shades of red, orange, and yellow, offering spectacular photo opportunities and pleasant hiking weather.

Accommodation

Today, Voskopojë is a good starting point for hikes, with numerous small hotels, restaurants, and private accommodations catering to tourists seeking peace and quiet amidst stunning natural surroundings.

Plan Your Visit

Essential Information for Your Journey to Voskopojë

🗓️ Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Voskopojë is during the spring and autumn months, when the weather is pleasant and the scenery is at its most stunning.

  • Spring: Witness vibrant blooms of wildflowers covering the mountainsides
  • Autumn: Experience hillsides painted with shades of red, orange, and yellow

Note: Both seasons offer comfortable temperatures ideal for hiking and exploring.

🚗 Getting There

From Tirana:

  1. Travel to Korçë (approximately 185 km from Tirana)
  2. Buses regularly link Korçë to Tirana and other cities
  3. From Korçë, take a local bus or taxi to Voskopojë (21 km)

From Korçë:

  • Distance: 21 kilometers
  • Transport options: Local bus or taxi
  • Travel time: Approximately 30-40 minutes

🏨 Accommodation

Voskopojë offers various accommodation options for visitors:

  • Small hotels with traditional charm
  • Guest houses and private accommodations
  • Restaurants serving local cuisine

Atmosphere: Most accommodations cater to tourists seeking peace, quiet, and an authentic mountain village experience.

ℹ️ What to Expect

  • Altitude: The village sits at 1,160m elevation - temperatures can be cooler than coastal areas
  • Terrain: Mountainous with some walking required to explore churches and trails
  • Pace: Relaxed mountain village atmosphere - ideal for those seeking tranquility
  • Activities: Church visits, hiking, nature walks, photography, and cultural exploration

💡 Travel Tips

  • Bring comfortable walking shoes for church visits and hiking trails
  • Pack layers - mountain weather can change quickly
  • Bring a camera - the scenery and frescoes are spectacular
  • Respect the historic churches - they are active preservation sites
  • Allow at least a full day to explore the village and its surroundings
  • Consider staying overnight to fully experience the mountain atmosphere
